RAKETENSPIEL 42 by Babuni.dev — VERSION 1.42

Maker: Babuni.dev (https://babuni.dev)

Run: Raketenspiel 42 v1.42 Portable.exe

Copy the EXE to a USB stick or any folder on your computer and double-click it.
No installation, browser, Flash, or internet connection is needed.
The first launch may take a few seconds while the runtime unpacks.

This is the updated game, including the latest laser changes, special asteroids,
high scores, menu instructions, and animation refinements from the browser source.

HIGH SCORES
The game creates a "Raketenspiel 42 Data" folder next to the EXE.
To take your scores with you, close the game and copy BOTH the EXE and this folder.
Copying just the EXE gives you a fresh high-score table at the new location.
If the USB stick or folder is read-only, scores use Windows user-data storage.
Existing Chrome/Edge/browser-preview scores are separate from the app's scores.

CONTROLS
Move the mouse to steer. Hold the left mouse button to fire.
Blue special asteroid: collect for shield. Green: collect for laser energy.
Red: shoot it down. At game over, enter your name and press Enter to save.
Close the window to exit. Close the game before removing a USB stick.

64-bit Windows build. User-facing version 1.42 (file/package version 1.42.0).
The executable is unsigned; Windows may display an unknown-publisher warning.
There is no setup program for this release.
